Notes & Behavior
  • Words are sequences of Unicode letters/marks/numbers. Diacritics are handled as well.
  • Every non-alphanumeric character (including space, newline, punctuation, symbols) is stored as a single token.
  • Capitalization memory only tracks whether the first grapheme of a word was uppercase; internal capitalization (e.g. iPhone) is not preserved.
  • If fewer words are provided during reconstruction than the original word tokens, missing words are treated as empty strings.
